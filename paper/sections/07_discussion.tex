\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The final discussion will distinguish three questions: whether the wireless system accurately represents vehicle motion, whether synchronized cues affect discomfort under the tested conditions, and whether any observed benefit is practically useful during laptop work. A null user effect would not invalidate the engineering contribution, and an apparent user benefit would not compensate for excessive delay or unsafe stale-cue behavior.

Expected limitations include a small exploratory sample, one primary vehicle and route, a narrow age range, sensor differences between only two phone models, imperfect reference instrumentation, incomplete participant blinding, and possible interruption from repeated symptom prompts. MotionCue will not be described as a medical device or guaranteed treatment.
