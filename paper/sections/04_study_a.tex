\section{Study A: Signal and System Validation}
\label{sec:study-a}

Study A will validate the sensing and communication chain before any effectiveness claims are made. The initial devices are a Samsung Galaxy A50 running Android~11 and an iPhone~16~Pro running the latest stable iOS available at testing. Exact model identifiers, OS builds, application commits, protocol version, sensor names, negotiated GATT PDU, and mount configuration will be recorded.

The phones will be fixed to a common rigid mount in the same passenger vehicle. Repeated maneuvers will include stationary periods, acceleration, braking, left and right turns, and stop--resume transitions. If a dedicated reference IMU is unavailable, the limitation of using a fixed comparison phone rather than metrological ground truth will be stated explicitly.

Primary engineering measures are axis agreement, event-detection precision and recall, end-to-end latency, jitter, sequence-derived packet loss, invalid-frame rate, reconnection time, neutralization time, and false-cue rate. BLE is the required first transport. A local Wi-Fi transport will be implemented only if BLE measurements justify the additional scope.

\textbf{Acceptance thresholds, route code, maneuver counts, and reference instrumentation are TBD and must be fixed before data collection.}
